The member resume book

Join the resume book.

Sponsors who fund Crypto Hogs get a book of members who want to be found. Put yourself in it and recruiters see you first. Optional, free, UARK students only, and you can leave any time.

01 / What it is One card. Your links.

A PDF the officers rebuild each semester: your name, major, graduation term, what you are into, and links to your LinkedIn and resume. No uploads. The links stay yours, so when you update the resume on Drive, the book points at the new one.

02 / Who sees it The people paying for the pizza.

Partner and Founding sponsors, the companies whose money covers speakers, events, and food, get the book for recruiting and nothing else. It is never posted online and never sold. Being in it means their recruiters see you before the career fair does.

03 / Your control Opt in. Opt out.

Completely optional, and it has nothing to do with membership, which stays free and open to every UA student. To change or remove your card, email uarkcryptohogs@gmail.com from your UARK address and it comes out of the next edition.
