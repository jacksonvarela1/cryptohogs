01 / Why we exist

Crypto is changing money, markets, and even Arkansas law. You'll work with it whether you plan to or not. Better to understand it now, together, than late, alone.

Our mission: position the University of Arkansas as a leading hub for digital-asset innovation. Educate students, connect them with industry leaders, and create opportunities in crypto, blockchain, and the future of finance. Crypto Hogs, official mission

06 / Zero gatekeeping

Common objections, answered.

The questions everyone asks before their first Tuesday, settled on-chain.

0x01 Do I need to own crypto? +

No. Most members start with zero. Curiosity is the only prerequisite, and it stays that way.

0x02 Do I need to be a finance or CS major? +

Every major is welcome. Crypto needs marketers, writers, policy minds, designers, and healthy skeptics just as much as it needs engineers.

0x03 Does it cost anything? +

$0. No dues, no paid course, no upsell, ever. Free pizza, on the other hand, happens regularly.

0x04 Is this financial advice? +

Emphatically not. We study the technology, the industry, and the policy around it. Your money stays yours and your decisions stay yours.

0x05 I know nothing about crypto. +

Perfect. The whole club is built for the curious, not the experts. Come confused, leave slightly less confused.
