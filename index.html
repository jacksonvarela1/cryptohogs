01 / Why we exist

Crypto is changing money, markets, and even Arkansas law. You'll work with it whether you plan to or not. Better to understand it now, together, than late, alone.

Our mission: position the University of Arkansas as a leading hub for digital-asset innovation. Educate students, connect them with industry leaders, and create opportunities in crypto, blockchain, and the future of finance. Crypto Hogs, official mission
02 / What we do

Four ways in.
Pick yours.

Whatever brought you here, a headline, a paycheck question, or a startup idea, there's a lane for it.

/ 01

Speaker nights

Founders, executives, miners, and policy leaders. On campus, in person, taking questions. Free and open to every UA student.

/ 02

Education

Meetings that make blockchain make sense: no prerequisites, no jargon walls, no finance degree required. Bring questions.

/ 03

Networking & careers

Connections with investors, builders, and people working full-time in the industry, plus internship pipelines and conference trips.

/ 04

Policy & innovation

Arkansas is writing crypto law right now. We track it, meet the people writing it, and put students in the room where it happens.
