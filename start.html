Start here

Never owned a single coin?

Neither had most of us. Below is the path we actually hand people who ask where to begin: five free things, in order, that take you from knowing nothing to holding your own in the room. Then come to a Tuesday and ask about the part you are still stuck on.

The path

Five things, in this order.

You do not need all five to show up. Step one alone is enough to follow a whole speaker night. Nothing here costs money and nothing here asks you to open an account.

Step 01 What this stuff actually is The Ethereum Foundation's own explainer, written for people who have never touched a wallet. It defines the words everyone else assumes you already know.ethereum.org 10 min
Step 02 The nine pages that started it Satoshi Nakamoto's 2008 paper, the original. You will not follow every line the first time, and that is normal. Read it anyway, because most arguments you hear later trace straight back to it.bitcoin.org · PDF 30 min
Step 03 Learn the scams before they find you Do this one before you ever move a dollar. The Federal Trade Commission lists the exact plays being run, and college students get hit with all of them.consumer.ftc.gov 10 min
Step 04 A whole MIT course, for free Blockchain and Money, taught at MIT Sloan by Prof. Gary Gensler. The lecture videos, readings and slides are posted in full on OpenCourseWare.ocw.mit.edu A semester, your pace
Step 05 See how the machine works Mastering Bitcoin, the technical book, free and open source in full on GitHub. Pick it up when "but how does that actually work" starts bothering you.github.com · open source When you are ready
No exchange links No referral codes Nothing to buy Not financial advice

We picked these because they are free, primary and unsponsored. Nobody paid to be on this list and nobody can.

Then what
Step six is a room.

Reading gets you the vocabulary. Sitting in a Tuesday gets you the part reading cannot: somebody to ask. Meetings run 5:30 to 7:00 PM CT, free and open to all University of Arkansas students, member or not.
